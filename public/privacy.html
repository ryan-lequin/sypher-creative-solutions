SYPHER / CONTACT PRIVACY

How your project inquiry is handled.

When you use our contact form, this website securely stores your name, email, selected service, optional budget and message in a private Supabase database, accessed by the website’s server API. Only someone signed in to the password-protected Studio Admin can view or change those records.

Purpose

Your details are used to review the inquiry, follow up about your project, and track its status. The site does not sell these details or publish them as testimonials.

Storage and access

Inquiries and administrator sessions are stored in private Supabase tables through the Vercel API. They are not automatically emailed. Administrators can view, export, update or delete inquiries. An administrator signs in using an HTTP-only cookie. The hosting policies of Vercel and Supabase also apply.

Email replies

By default, the administrator uses a Gmail compose link that includes the customer email address and the draft subject and body. This opens Google Gmail in a new tab; the administrator must click Send manually. The site cannot confirm when a Gmail reply has been sent and does not automatically store Gmail reply history. Administrators update inquiry status manually after sending. Historical Resend reply records may be visible if they were migrated from earlier versions.

Requests or corrections

To request access, correction or deletion of an inquiry, use the contact information displayed on the home page, if configured. The website owner must add their actual business contact details and applicable retention period before publicly deploying this project.

This notice describes the supplied software's default behavior. Site owners are responsible for configuring real business details and reviewing applicable privacy requirements.
